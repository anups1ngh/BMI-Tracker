Privacy Policy

Introduction

Thank you for choosing FitTrack. This Privacy Policy describes how the App collects, uses, and shares information when you use our services. By installing and using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

To use FitTrack's core features, you must create an account. We collect personal information such as your email address, password, and basic profile details (e.g., age, height, weight, and fitness goals) to personalize your experience.

1.2 Health and Fitness Data

With your explicit permission, FitTrack accesses health and physical activity data, including step counts, active minutes, and calories burned, through integration with Apple Health (HealthKit) and Google Health Connect.

2. Apple HealthKit & Google Health Connect

FitTrack respects the highly sensitive nature of your health data. When integrating with Apple HealthKit and Google Health Connect, we strictly adhere to the following principles:

  • We only read or write data to these services with your explicit consent.
  • Data accessed from HealthKit and Health Connect is used solely to provide health, motion, and fitness-related services within the FitTrack dashboard and AI Coach features.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your HealthKit or Health Connect data with third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Personalize your dashboard, meal plans, and fitness goals.
  • Power our AI Coach, which analyzes your historical data to provide dynamic, personalized health insights.
  • Send you push notifications (such as water reminders and goal achievements), which you can opt out of at any time in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services

FitTrack uses trusted third-party services to operate efficiently. These services are bound by strict privacy and security policies:

  • Google Firebase: Used for secure user authentication, database storage (Firestore), and push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Vertex AI / Gemini (Google): Used to generate intelligent responses for the AI Coach. Your data is processed securely and is not used to train public machine learning models.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You maintain full control over your data. You can delete your account and all associated personal and health data at any time directly within the FitTrack app settings. Upon account deletion, your authentication records, daily metrics, and meal logs are permanently removed from our active databases.

6. Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including data encryption in transit and at rest, to protect your personal information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children’s Privacy

FitTrack is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete it.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the App. Your continued use of the App after any such modification constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
